➤ **3.1** Everyone will receive two new small **PCR reaction tube** labelled with their previously assigned sample number and a PCR raction tube for the 6× PCR master mix (step 3.2). Place the reaction tubes in a tube rack.

➤ **3.2** One person per lab group uses 10 µL and 100 µL/200 µL pipettes to **mix the 6× PCR master mix (Table 1; third column)** for one primer pair. Another person from your group will prepare the 6× PCR master mix for the other primer pair. Label the primer on the lit of the reaction tube to avoid confusion!

> [!IMPORTANT]
> We have two different mitochondrial DNA (mtDNA) markers (Teleo02 and Vert 16S; see NOTE below). The pipetting scheme (Table 1) is the same for both primer pairs but is done seperatly!
>
> Only one 6× PCR master mix per primer pair (Teleo02 and Vert 16S) *and* per lab group (4 people) is needed. Each person will prepare their own two PCR reactions later (step 3.5).

> ***Table 1:** Components and volumes used for a single PCR reaction and for a master mix prepared for six reactions. The DNA extract ( * ) is obtained from the supernatant collected in step 2.11 and is added separately to each PCR reaction. The group's 6× PCR master mix is prepared for six reactions to account for pipetting errors and ensure sufficient volume for all four PCR reactions per marker.*
> 
> | Component                          | Volume per PCR reaction (step 3.5) | Volume for 6× PCR master mix (step 3.2) |
> | ---------------------------------- | ---------------------------------: | --------------------------------------: |
> | VWR® Taq DNA Polymerase Master Mix |                              10 µL |                                   60 µL |
> | Forward (F) primer                 |                             0.4 µL |                                  2.4 µL |

➤ **3.5** Everybody take the new PCR reaction tubes labelled with their previously assigned sample number and uses a 10 µL pipette to mix the final PCR reaction per primer pair each. **Mix 17 µL PCR master mix (containing Teleo02) with 1 µL DNA extract** and **mix 17 µL PCR master mix (containing Vert 16S) with 1 µL DNA extract (Table 1; second column)**. Label the primer on the lit of the reaction tube to avoid confusion!

> [!NOTE]
> In our course, we use two different primer pairs:
> - **Teleo02**: A widely used fish-specific primer pair often employed in environmental DNA (eDNA) metabarcoding to detect, identify, and monitor freshwater and marine bony fish (teleost) biodiversity. It targets a hypervariable region of the mitochondrial 12S rRNA gene, resulting in a roughly 170 base pairs (bp) long fragment amplified during PCR.
> - **Vert 16S**: A universal vertebrate primer pair often employed in eDNA metabarcoding. It targets a hypervariable region of the mitochondrial 16S rRNA gene, resulting in a roughly 250 bp long fragment amplified during PCR.
>
> Each primer pair comes with the forward primer (F) and the reverse primer (R) in seperate tubes, which you pipette together into the PCR reaction (step 3.2).

➤ **3.6** Close the lit and vortex the PCR reaction tubes with the PCR reaction.

➤ **3.7** Quick spin-down at maximum speed in the Gyro™ Plus Microcentrifuge (Figure 5).

➤ **3.9** Start the PCR programme (Table 2).

> ***Table 2:** PCR programme for both primer pairs.*
> 
> | PCR Step             | Temperature |   Time | Cycles |
> | -------------------- | ----------: | -----: | -----: |
> | Initial denaturation |       95 °C |  900 s |      1 |
> | Denaturation         |       95 °C |   30 s |     30 |
> | Annealing            |       63 °C |   60 s |     30 |
> | Extension            |       72 °C |   45 s |     30 |
> | Final extension      |       72 °C |  600 s |      1 |
> | Hold                 |        4 °C |      ∞ |      — |

➤ **3.10** Quick spin-down at maximum speed in the Gyro™ Plus Microcentrifuge (Figure 5).
