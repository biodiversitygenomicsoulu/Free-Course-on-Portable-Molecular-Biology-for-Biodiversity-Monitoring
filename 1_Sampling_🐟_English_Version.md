> [!NOTE]
> Proper documentation of information about a sample during collection is essential for scientific research. Molecular data cannot be used effectively in scientific research if the information about the sample was not recorded or has been lost. In that case, your laboratory work may have been a waste of time. Always think before collecting a sample about what information might become important for your research, and record this information along with the sample. When in doubt, collect more information than you expect to need. Take the *Collection date* and *Collection location*, for example. Of course, we collected all the fish for the course on the same day and at the same fish market in Bissau. Nevertheless, the date and location are essential standard information for any kind of scientific collection.
