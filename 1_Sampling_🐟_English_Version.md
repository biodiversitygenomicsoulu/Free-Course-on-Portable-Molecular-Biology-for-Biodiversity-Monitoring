> [!NOTE]
> The documentation of information on the sample during collection for scientific research is very important. Molecular data cannot be used in scientific research if you did not record or lost the data on the sample and your labwork was a waste of time. Always think about what kind of information might become important for your research **before** and collect it with the sample. When in doubt, gather more information than you expect to need. Like in our case with the *Collection date* and *Collection location*: of course we collected all fish for the course at the same day at the same fish market in Bissau, but time and location are anessential standart information for any kind of scientific collection.
